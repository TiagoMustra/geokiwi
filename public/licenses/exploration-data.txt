Exploration catalogue

Natural Earth vector geographic data, version v5.1.2.
Public domain. https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Made with Natural Earth. https://www.naturalearthdata.com/

Wikidata structured coordinate data (P625).
CC0 / public domain. https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing
Each used entity is linked from its catalogue item and listed in
  docs/exploration-data-provenance.json

Geographic polygons and river lines are generalized cartographic representations,
not legal, cadastral or navigation boundaries. All selected source vertices and
holes are retained, with coordinates rounded to 5 decimal places.

Names translated and brief descriptions written for GeoKiwi. No third-party
photographs, map tiles or other media are included in this dataset.
