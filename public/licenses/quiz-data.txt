GeoKiwi Conhecimento geral / General knowledge — data attribution (2026-11-06)

Original Portuguese/English question text accompanies individually linked sources.

Common country names, land-border snapshot and inland/coastal status:
Mledoze / Countries, ODbL 1.0
https://github.com/mledoze/countries
https://github.com/mledoze/countries/blob/master/LICENSE
Snapshot details: src/quiz/borders-snapshot.json and docs/quiz-bank.md.

City coordinates:
Natural Earth v5.1.2, public domain
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/v5.1.2
Monument and summit coordinates: Wikidata, CC0
https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing

Population/surface-area observations:
World Bank World Development Indicators, with indicator/source definitions and
reference years linked in each question. Canada total area uses Statistics Canada.
https://data.worldbank.org/summary-terms-of-use

History, heights, distances and island counts cite the responsible institutional
sources individually. Full verification notes are in docs/quiz-history-provenance.md,
docs/quiz-numbers-provenance.md and docs/quiz-boolean-provenance.md.

All answers are bundled snapshots. No statistics are fetched at game time.

Flag-pattern questions: official national symbol descriptions linked in each question,
with bounded sets checked against the bundled flag-icons collection (MIT).
https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons
Verification: docs/quiz-flags-provenance.md.

Monument photography: individual creators and Creative Commons licenses.
Full photo credits: /licenses/landmark-photos.txt

Continental, flag and world variants: verification notes in
docs/quiz-continental-provenance.md, docs/quiz-flag-variants-provenance.md and
docs/quiz-world-variants-provenance.md. Sources are attached to every question
and redistributed in /downloads/conhecimento-geral.md and .json.
